Support

Help for the Real Dental Costs plugin and connector for AI assistants, served from https://mcp.realdentalcosts.com/mcp (ChatGPT and other clients) and https://mcp.realdentalcosts.com/claude (Claude).

Contact

Email research@realdentalcosts.com. We answer by email, usually within a few working days.

Report a wrong figure

Each figure in an answer carries its source URL and period. To report one that looks wrong, send:

  • the US state (and the city, county or CDT code if any);
  • the figure concerned, or the tool and the question asked;
  • a link to the source that shows the right figure, if you have one.

We check it against the source. A confirmed error is corrected in the dataset, with a new date, on realdentalcosts.com and in the tools.

Common questions

"no_source_at_this_level". No published source covers that figure at that level (state, city). The answer then gives the next level up, such as the national figure, and says so.

Estimated or observed. Each figure carries a badge: observed means measured, estimated means modelled from a measured series. The method is on the methodology page.

Fee schedule age scope. An official Medicaid fee schedule is marked children_only when its source says so and not_verified when the source does not state which ages it covers. It is shown for context only.

Other countries. The tools cover the United States. They give no figures for other countries.

Advice. Real Dental Costs publishes market research, not quotes and not medical advice. It does not recommend dentists or insurers.
