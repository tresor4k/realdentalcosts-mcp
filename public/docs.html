Real Dental Costs for AI assistants

Real Dental Costs gives an AI assistant sourced US dental cost research: what a procedure costs in cash by state and nationally, what Medicaid paid per claim line, what each state's adult Medicaid dental benefit covers, and where low-cost care exists. It is a remote server of the Model Context Protocol (MCP), read-only, with no account and no authentication. Publisher: Real Dental Costs (realdentalcosts.com).

Server URLs (Streamable HTTP, POST, JSON responses):

  • https://mcp.realdentalcosts.com/mcp for ChatGPT and other MCP clients;
  • https://mcp.realdentalcosts.com/claude for Claude.

Both serve the same tools and figures. Links in answers carry utm_source=chatgpt or utm_source=claude.

Tools

All tools are read-only (readOnlyHint: true, destructiveHint: false, openWorldHint: false) and accept lang en or es.

  • dental_cost_by_state: the cash band (low, average, high) of one of 12 procedures in a US state (51 incl. DC), the national band, the gap in percent and the state's Restorative Cost Index, with an observed or estimated badge. For crown, veneer, braces and dentures: no state figure (see Observed or estimated), the national band instead.
  • dental_national_average: the national cash band of a procedure and the states at both ends of the range of state averages.
  • benchmark_treatment_plan: a treatment plan checked line by line (CDT codes or procedure names, quantities, quoted amounts): state cash band (national band for crown, veneer, braces and dentures) and where the quoted amount falls in it, what Medicaid paid per claim line, the official fee schedule with its age scope, the state's adult Medicaid rule and numbered sources.
  • medicaid_adult_dental_rules: a state's adult Medicaid dental level, annual dollar cap with a verbatim quote, orthodontics and implants policy, share of dentists billing Medicaid and the official fee schedule.
  • medicaid_paid_by_code: what Medicaid paid per claim line (T-MSIS 2018-2024) for one of 61 CDT codes, nationally or in a state, with the official fee when one is published.
  • low_cost_dental_options: dental schools, health centers, dental shortage areas (HPSA), Mission of Mercy events and charity programs in a state, with an optional county.

Each answer has a structuredContent object; the treatment-plan tools return a numbered list of sources. In ChatGPT and Claude, a card shows the figures and its sources.

Example questions

  • "How much is a root canal in Ohio without insurance?"
  • "My dentist quoted D2740 and four quadrants of D4341 in Arizona. Is each line in the usual range?"
  • "Does Medicaid cover dental care for adults in Georgia, and is there a yearly limit?"
  • "¿Cuánto paga Medicaid por el código D7140 en Florida?"
  • "Where can I find a dental school clinic in Ohio?"

How to read the figures

  • Source and period. Every figure carries the URL of its source and its period or effective date.
  • Observed or estimated. Observed figures are measured. Estimated figures are not collected prices: the state implant series is an editorial estimate (edition 2026.1; state ranking validated against ASQ360, levels not verified), and other estimated figures are scaled from a national range by an index built on it. Band edges (low, high) are modelled.
  • Figures not published here. The per-state averages for crown, veneer, braces and dentures come from the ASQ360/CareCredit Average Procedural Cost Study 2023-2024. They are cited with attribution on realdentalcosts.com pages but are not licensed for redistribution: the tools return no_source_at_this_level, the study link and the national band.
  • Missing figures. When no source covers a level, the answer says no_source_at_this_level and gives the next level up. Nothing is invented.
  • Medicaid paid per claim line is what Medicaid paid (T-MSIS), not what a patient is charged.
  • Official fee schedules carry an age scope: children_only when the source says so, not_verified otherwise. They are context only and never the reference for where a quoted amount falls.

Limits

The tools cover the United States only. They do not diagnose, choose a treatment, give a named insurer's plan terms or recommend a dentist. Market research, not quotes, not medical advice. Method: realdentalcosts.com/en/methodology.
