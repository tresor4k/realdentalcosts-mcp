What the service is

A read-only server of the Model Context Protocol that returns published dental cost research for the United States and Canada. In the United States: cash bands by state, city and nationally, what Medicaid paid per claim line, official Medicaid fee schedules, adult Medicaid dental rules, Medicare dental rules, an out-of-pocket estimate from your own plan figures, dated program changes and low-cost care routes. In Canada: costs by province and a Canadian Dental Care Plan co-pay check. It is free to use and needs no account.

Not advice, not a quote

The figures are observed market research and public program data, each with its source and period. They are not a quote from any dentist, not a statement of what an insurer or Medicaid will pay for a given person, and not medical, dental, coding, insurance or financial advice. A dentist's estimate and the person's own coverage documents decide what a treatment costs. Real Dental Costs does not recommend dentists, clinics or insurers.

Accuracy

Figures are checked against their sources and dated. They can still be wrong or out of date; a figure without a source at a given level is reported as missing instead of being estimated. Please report a wrong figure through the support page.

Acceptable use

Do not send personal or health information in tool arguments, do not overload the server, and do not present its figures as a quote or as advice. Reuse of the underlying open datasets follows the licence stated on each dataset page of realdentalcosts.com.
